T-Cash Card Guide

How to get the card?

What is the fee?

Total Rs 275 = Rs 130 card + Rs 130 delivery + Rs 15 service charges. Pay via BOP app, any bank app/ATM/branch, JazzCash or Easypaisa. Card validity is 5 years.

How to recharge?

1) Fund transfer from the BOP Mobile App, or 2) IBFT from any bank — the T-Cash card number is the account number. Standard IBFT charges apply.

Important

Money in the physical T-Cash Card and the app Virtual Wallet is SEPARATE — it cannot be transferred between them. The Virtual Wallet is free and transit-only; the physical card also works at ATMs/POS.

What is the bus fare?

As per the Punjab government e-bus policy (punjab.gov.pk), the fare is a flat Rs 20, and travel is completely FREE for students, senior citizens and special persons.
