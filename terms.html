RYK Electric Bus is a free guide to the PTC Electric Bus timetable for Rahim Yar Khan. By using the app or the website, you accept these terms.

What this app is — and is not

This app is not a bus service and is not operated by Punjab Transport Company (PTC). It only presents PTC timetable information, plus extra tools for passengers: a trip planner, alarms, nearest-stop search, offline timings and passenger-submitted reports. The app does not provide live tracking: all times are scheduled times, and a bus may run late.

Timetable data and fares

Routes, stops and times come from official PTC timetable sources and are shown as published. Fares follow PTC: Rs 20 flat, with free travel for students, elderly and special passengers as per PTC rules. For critical travel, verify times and fares against current official information.

Passenger reports

Reports in the app are submitted by passengers using ready-made options. They are not verified by us and may be inaccurate. Reports contain no free text and no personal information.

Availability

The app works offline with bundled timetable data and updates when it is online. We do not guarantee uninterrupted service, and timetable data can change when PTC changes it.

Liability

We are not liable for missed buses, delays, or decisions made using this app. Always confirm important trips against official sources.
