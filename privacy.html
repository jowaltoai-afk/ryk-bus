RYK Electric Bus — Privacy Policy

App (com.rykbus.app) and website rykbus.dpdns.org · Effective: 20 October 2026

RYK Electric Bus is a free community app that shows the PTC Electric Bus timetable for Rahim Yar Khan. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to run the service.

What we collect

  • Anonymous usage counts. The app counts app launches and first-time installs, and the website counts visits and app downloads, using Google Analytics with a random, app-generated ID. This ID is not linked to your name, phone number, email or any account.
  • Bus reports. If you submit a report (bus late, missed, crowded, on time or an alert), the route, stop, time and the ready-made option you picked are shared publicly inside the app so other passengers can see them. Reports contain no free text and no personal information.

Location

Your location is used only on your own device to find your nearest bus stop and walking distance. Your location is never sent to our servers and never stored. If you tap a Map button, the stop location opens in Google Maps, which has its own privacy policy.

What we do NOT collect

  • No accounts or sign-up in the user app — we never ask for your name, phone number, email or CNIC.
  • No contacts, photos, files, call logs or messages.
  • No advertising IDs and no ads. We do not sell or share data with advertisers.

Data storage and security

Anonymous counters and bus reports are stored on our service provider (Cloudflare) and analytics are processed by Google Analytics. Timetable data shown in the app comes from the official eTransit Punjab journey planner.

Your choices

You can use the app without location permission by choosing your stop manually. If you believe a bus report concerns you, or you have any privacy question, contact us and we will remove it or help you.
